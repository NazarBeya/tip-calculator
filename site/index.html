Privacy Policy

Tip Calculator · Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short

Tip Calculator is a 100% offline utility. It collects, stores, tracks and shares no personal data and no device information.

About the app

Tip Calculator (package com.smartcalc.tipcalc) is an Android app that calculates tips and splits a bill between several people. It is developed by SmartCalc (“we”, “us”).

No internet access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot connect to any network. It has no backend, no accounts and no advertising.

Data we collect

None. In particular, the app does not collect, store, track or transmit:

  • personal information (name, email, phone, location, contacts, photos or files);
  • device information or identifiers (advertising ID, IP address, device model);
  • usage, analytics or crash data.

The bill amount, tip percentage and number of people you enter are used only for the calculation on your screen. They are kept in memory while the app is open and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Third parties

Because no data is collected, none is shared with, sold to or processed by third parties. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.
